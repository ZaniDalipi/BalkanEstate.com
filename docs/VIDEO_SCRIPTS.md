## NARRATION STYLE GUIDE

### Tone
- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Conversational** like talking to a friend
- **Enthusiastic** but genuine
- **Clear** and easy to understand

### Pacing
- Short-form: Fast-paced, punchy
- Long-form: Natural, breathing room
- Always match the visual cuts

### Voice Characteristics (for AI voice selection)
- Male or female (both work)
- Age: 25-40 (professional but relatable)
- Accent: Neutral English or slight European
- Energy: Medium-high

### ElevenLabs Recommended Settings
- Stability: 0.50
- Clarity: 0.75
- Style exaggeration: 0.30
