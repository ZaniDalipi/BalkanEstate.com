02 — The Problem

The Balkan Property Market Is Broken

🌐

Fragmented Across 10 Countries

Buyers must navigate dozens of local portals — each with different languages, formats, and standards. No unified experience exists.

🚧

Language Barriers Block Buyers

Cross-border property search is nearly impossible. International buyers can't navigate local portals in unfamiliar languages.

🔍

No Verification or Trust

Buyers have no way to verify agent credentials. Fraud and misrepresentation are common pain points across the region.

📱

Outdated, Slow Platforms

Existing portals run on old tech — poor mobile experience, no real-time communication, no smart tools. The market deserves better.
