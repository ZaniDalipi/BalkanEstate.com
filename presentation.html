02 — The Problem

The Balkan Real Estate Market Is Broken

A massive market with no modern, unified platform to serve it.

🌐

Fragmented Market

Buyers must jump between dozens of local portals across different countries — each with different languages, formats, and standards.

🚧

Language Barriers

Cross-border property search is nearly impossible. International buyers can't navigate local portals in unfamiliar languages.

🔍

No Trust or Verification

Buyers have no way to verify agent credentials or reliability. Fraud and misrepresentation are common pain points.

📱

Outdated Platforms

Existing portals are built on old technology — poor mobile experience, slow, no smart tools, no real-time communication.
